Cubie Away does not require a player account, and bpuzzle does not currently operate a cloud-save or player-profile backend. Gameplay information is stored locally on your device. Advertising, consent, app-store, and purchase providers may process information as explained below.

1. Scope and who is responsible

This Privacy Policy applies to Cubie Away: 3D Block Puzzle (the “Game”), published by bpuzzle. For information handled directly by bpuzzle, bpuzzle is responsible for that processing. Third-party providers may separately be responsible for information they process under their own privacy policies.

2. Information stored by the Game

The Game may store level progress, an unfinished level state, move counts, virtual currency, booster inventory, tutorial progress, audio and vibration preferences, and purchase-entitlement status locally on your device. This information supports gameplay, restores an unfinished attempt, remembers settings, and prevents duplicate rewards or purchases.

bpuzzle does not currently receive this local gameplay information through a player account or cloud-save service. You can normally remove it by clearing the Game’s app data or uninstalling the Game. Store-confirmed purchase entitlements may be restored again from your store account.

3. Advertising and consent

The Game uses Unity LevelPlay mediation to provide interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads. Ad demand may come from services including Unity Ads, ironSource Exchange, and Google AdMob. Depending on your device, region, privacy choices, and the provider serving an ad, these services may process:

  • Advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device model, operating system, language, screen, and app information;
  • Ad requests, availability, impressions, views, clicks, rewards, and revenue measurement;
  • Network, performance, security, fraud-prevention, and diagnostic information.

Where required, the Game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy choices before advertising requests are enabled. Your choices may affect whether personalized, non-personalized, limited, or technical ad delivery is available; declining personalized advertising does not necessarily mean that no ads will be shown. Where available, you can revisit applicable choices through Privacy Options in the Game’s Settings menu.

4. In-app purchases

The Game may offer a non-consumable “Remove Ads” purchase. Purchases are processed by the platform store, such as Google Play, with Unity In-App Purchasing integration. Product identifiers, purchase status, transaction identifiers, receipts, and entitlement information may be processed to complete, verify, acknowledge, and restore purchases. bpuzzle does not receive or store your complete payment-card details.

“Remove Ads” removes forced interstitial ads. It does not remove optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit.

5. Support communications

If you contact support, bpuzzle receives your email address, message, and any device details, screenshots, or attachments you choose to provide. This information is used to answer your request, investigate the issue, and maintain necessary support records. Please do not send passwords, complete payment-card details, or unrelated sensitive information.

6. Why information is processed

Information may be processed to operate and secure the Game, preserve progress and settings, deliver and measure ads, provide earned rewarded-ad benefits, prevent fraud and duplicate transactions, diagnose failures, process purchases, restore entitlements, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations.

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, processing may rely on performance of a service you requested, consent, compliance with law, or legitimate interests such as operating, securing, and improving the Game and preventing fraud. You may withdraw consent through the available privacy controls without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

7. Service providers and sharing

bpuzzle does not sell personal information for money. Advertising technology may involve disclosure or use of identifiers and activity data that some laws describe as “sharing” or “targeted advertising.” Applicable choices are provided through the consent or privacy-options flow where required.

Relevant third-party policies include:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

8. Retention and security

Local gameplay information remains on your device until it is reset, cleared, or removed with the Game. Support correspondence is kept only for as long as reasonably needed to resolve requests, keep necessary records, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. Advertising and store providers apply their own retention rules.

bpuzzle uses reasonable safeguards appropriate to the Game, but no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children’s privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. bpuzzle does not knowingly ask children to create an account or directly submit their name or email address through the Game. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to bpuzzle, contact us so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, or withdrawal of consent, and to appeal or complain to a data-protection authority. Residents of certain U.S. states may also have rights concerning targeted advertising, sale, or sharing and freedom from discriminatory treatment for exercising their rights.

You can clear locally stored gameplay data through your device settings, manage applicable advertising choices through the Game’s Privacy Options, and manage or reset your advertising identifier through device settings. For data controlled by an advertising or app-store provider, you may also need to contact that provider. To make a request to bpuzzle, use the email address below.

11. International processing

Advertising, platform, and support providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, the responsible provider uses legal safeguards for international transfers as described in its privacy policy.

12. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the Game, its service providers, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest version. Material changes will be communicated where required.

Cuando es obligatorio, el Juego utiliza Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) para presentar opciones de privacidad antes de habilitar solicitudes publicitarias. Tus opciones pueden determinar si están disponibles anuncios personalizados, no personalizados, limitados o una entrega meramente técnica. Cuando esté disponible, puedes revisar tus preferencias en Privacy Options dentro de los ajustes del Juego.

4. Compras dentro de la aplicación

El Juego puede ofrecer una compra única llamada «Remove Ads». Las compras son procesadas por la tienda de la plataforma, como Google Play, junto con la integración de Unity In-App Purchasing. Pueden tratarse identificadores de producto, estado de compra, identificadores de transacción, recibos e información de derechos para completar, verificar, confirmar y restaurar compras. bpuzzle no recibe ni almacena los datos completos de tu tarjeta de pago.

«Remove Ads» elimina los anuncios intersticiales forzados. No elimina los anuncios recompensados opcionales que decides ver a cambio de una ventaja dentro del Juego.
